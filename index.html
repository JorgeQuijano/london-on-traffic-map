About this data

The City of London publishes approximate AADT for each road segment, usually once a year. AADT is the average number of vehicles passing a point each day over a full year. Street averages here are weighted by segment length so a single short outlier cannot make an entire street look busier than it is.

Counts are shown as published. A few short segments contain apparent source anomalies. Use the segment popup to inspect the underlying value.
